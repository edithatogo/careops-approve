# Tenant-owner request: CareOps approval pilot

Use this checklist with a Microsoft 365 or Power Platform owner. Record the
response and evidence reference outside Git; do not add screenshots, exports,
UPNs, credentials, or live payloads to this repository.

## Confirm

- Teams Approvals is enabled for the pilot users.
- Microsoft Forms or an approved Teams submission surface is available.
- Standard Power Automate, SharePoint, and Approvals licensing is sufficient.
- Teams, Approvals, SharePoint, and Power Automate are permitted by DLP policy.
- The pilot environment and SharePoint retention/audit settings are approved.
- Pilot data is limited to approved non-sensitive content.
- Workflow owner, backup owner, requester, approver, and escalation owner are authorised.
- The flow may suppress email notifications and surface status in Teams/SharePoint.
- A 14-day timeout may escalate to the nominated EDMS approver.
- An approved desktop/intranet execution machine is available, or that phase is deferred.

## Evidence to return

- Decision date and approving role.
- Environment classification and name held outside Git.
- DLP/licensing confirmation reference.
- SharePoint retention/audit confirmation reference.
- Named owner and backup owner.
- Pilot start/end window.
- Any conditions, exclusions, or expiry/review date.
