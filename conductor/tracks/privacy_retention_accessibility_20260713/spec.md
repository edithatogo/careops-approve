# Privacy, retention and accessibility hardening

## Overview

Make the approval process usable on Teams web/mobile and assistive technology while
ensuring request visibility, audit retention and disposal remain least-privilege and
tenant-policy aligned.

## Requirements

- Define visibility by requester, assigned approver, authorised editor and owner.
- Validate accessible labels, keyboard operation, concise content and mobile layout.
- Record retention-policy ownership and evidence without changing tenant policy.
- Support future subject visibility only through an explicit policy-controlled role.

## Acceptance criteria

- Role visibility, access denial, keyboard, screen-reader labels and narrow-screen cases pass.
- Retention and disposal remain external tenant-owner gates where admin rights are required.

## Out of scope

- Compliance-center policy changes, tenant-wide sharing or sensitive-content publication.
