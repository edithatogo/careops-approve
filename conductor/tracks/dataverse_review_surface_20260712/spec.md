# Dataverse review surface

Assess the existing Document Submission Center and TESL Submission/Flow
Telemetry tables as a possible owner correction and reporting surface. Reuse
only tenant-neutral contracts and preserve native Teams Approvals as the
decision authority. Do not require Dataverse if standard SharePoint/Teams
connectors are sufficient or licensing/DLP is not approved.

Acceptance: a documented fit decision, field/permission mapping, redaction
boundary, and fallback path exist; any live activation has tenant evidence.
