# Teams, roster, and delegation integration

Use native Teams Approvals for decisions, with Teams-only status cards and
14-day EDMS escalation. Resolve primary, fallback, urgent delegate, and EDMS
roles prospectively from authorised configuration. Record verbal delegation and
never silently reassign an in-flight approval.

Acceptance: visibility, approve/reject, timeout, escalation, inactive-primary,
urgent-delegation, and owner-notification scenarios pass without email.
